The 30-night trial

A pillow can only be judged in bed, on your nights. So every Somnila pillow (Neck 01, Contour 01, Side 01, Body 01, Lounge 01) and every set that contains one comes with a 30-night trial, counted from the day it is delivered.

If it isn't right, send us an email within those 30 nights with your order number. We refund the price of the pillow to the payment method you used. No form, no questions beyond what would help us make a better pillow. You don't need to send the pillow back.

Accessories

Mask 01, Quiet 01, covers and Throw 01 are hygiene and textile items. They can be returned unused, in their packaging, within 14 days of delivery for a refund. Write to us first and we send the return instructions.

Defects

If a product arrives damaged or a defect appears in normal use, write to us with a photo and your order number. We replace it or refund it. This does not limit your statutory rights, for example the two-year legal guarantee of conformity in the European Union.

Refund timing

Refunds are issued as soon as we confirm the request by email. Depending on your bank, the amount appears on your statement within 5 to 10 business days.
